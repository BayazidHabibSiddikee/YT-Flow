# The 48 Laws of Power — Robert Greene (Condensed)

## Law 1: Never Outshine the Master
Make those above you feel comfortably superior. In their desire to feel important, they will not feel threatened by you. Always make those above you feel relaxed and outshine them in deeds but never in name.

## Law 2: Never Put Too Much Trust in Friends, Learn How to Use Enemies
Friends will betray you more quickly because they are easily aroused to envy. They become tyrannical and haughty. Hire former enemies — they prove more loyal because they have more to prove.

## Law 3: Conceal Your Intentions
Keep people off-balance and in the dark by never revealing the purpose behind your actions. If they have no clue what you are up to, they cannot prepare a defense.

## Law 4: Always Say Less Than Necessary
The more you say, the more common you appear and the less in control. Powerful people impress and intimidate by saying less.

## Law 5: So Much Depends on Reputation — Guard It with Your Life
Your reputation is the cornerstone of power. Once it is established, people will do most of the work for you.

## Law 6: Court Attention at All Costs
Stand out. Be conspicuous at all costs. Make yourself a magnet of attention by appearing mysterious, extraordinary, or daring.

## Law 7: Get Others to Do the Work for You, but Always Take the Credit
Save your time and energy. Use other people's knowledge and labor to advance your own cause.

## Law 8: Make Other People Come to You — Use Bait if Necessary
When you force the other person to act, you are the one in control. Always turn your own weaknesses into traps.

## Law 9: Win Through Your Actions, Never Through Argument
Any momentary victory you think you have gained through argument is a Pyrrhic victory. The resentment you create is stronger and lasts longer.

## Law 10: Infection: Avoid the Unhappy and Unlucky
Emotional states are as infectious as diseases. Association with the unhappy and unlucky can destroy you.

## Law 11: Learn to Keep People Dependent on You
To maintain your independence you must always be needed and wanted. The more you are relied on, the more freedom you have.

## Law 12: Use Selective Honesty and Generosity to Disarm Your Victim
One sincere and honest move will cover over dozens of dishonest ones.

## Law 13: When Asking for Help, Appeal to People's Self-Interest, Never to their Mercy
If you need help from someone, never remind them of your past assistance. Instead, uncover something in your request that will benefit them.

## Law 14: Pose as a Friend, Work as a Spy
Knowing about your rival is critical. Use spies to gather information. But the best spies are those who blend in with the crowd.

## Law 15: Crush Your Enemy Totally
If you leave one ember alive, it will eventually burst into flame. Complete destruction is the only way to end a conflict.

## Law 16: Use Absence to Increase Respect and Honor
Too much circulation makes the price go down. The more you are seen and heard from, the more common you appear. Appear rarely to increase your perceived value.

## Law 17: Keep Others in Suspended Terror — Cultivate an Air of Unpredictability
Humans are creatures of habit with an insatiable need to see familiarity in other people's actions. Your predictability gives them a sense of control. Turn the tables — be unpredictable.

## Law 18: Do Not Build Fortresses to Protect Yourself — Isolation is Dangerous
Isolation cuts you off from valuable information and makes you an easy target. Circulate among people, find allies, mingle.

## Law 19: Know Who You're Dealing With — Do Not Offend the Wrong Person
There are many different kinds of people in the world. Do not mistakenly deal with the wrong one.

## Law 20: Do Not Commit to Anyone
It is the fool who always rushes to take sides. Do not commit to anyone — remain neutral and appear to be the one who agrees with everyone.

## Law 21: Play a Sucker to Catch a Sucker — Seem Dumber Than Your Mark
No one likes feeling stupider than the next person. The trick is to make the other person feel smart.

## Law 22: Use the Surrender Tactic: Transform Weakness into Power
When you are weaker, surrender rather than fight. The surrender gives you time to recover and plot.

## Law 23: Concentrate Your Forces
Conserve your forces. Focus your energy on your most important targets. Hoard your best assets.

## Law 24: Play the Perfect Courtier
The perfect courtier thrives in a world where everything revolves around power and political skill. They adapt to different situations, say less than necessary, and never challenge the master.

## Law 25: Re-Create Yourself
Do not accept the roles that society foists on you. Re-create yourself by forging a new identity.

## Law 26: Keep Your Hands Clean
You must seem a paragon of civility and efficiency. Let others do the dirty work. Always appear positive and cooperative.

## Law 27: Play on People's Need to Believe to Create a Cultlike Following
People have an overwhelming desire to believe in something. Give them a cause, a purpose, a belief.

## Law 28: Enter Action with Boldness
If you are unsure of a course of action, do not attempt it. Timidity is dangerous. Boldness is the key.

## Law 29: Plan All the Way to the End
Think several steps ahead. Consider all consequences. Do not let your plans be limited by fears of what might happen.

## Law 30: Make Your Accomplishments Seem Effortless
Your actions must seem natural and executed with ease. Display the effortlessness of the master.

## Law 31: Control the Options — Get Others to Play with the Cards You Deal
Give people options that all come out in your favor. The victim will choose the option you want.

## Law 32: Play to People's Fantasies
The truth is often ugly and unpleasant. Never appeal to truth unless you are prepared for the anger that comes from disillusionment. Appeal to fantasy.

## Law 33: Discover Each Man's Thumbscrew
Everyone has a weakness — a gap in the castle wall. It is usually something they are ashamed of or want to hide.

## Law 34: Be Royal in Your Own Fashion — Act Like a King to Be Treated Like One
Act with nobility and dignity. People will treat you accordingly.

## Law 35: Master the Art of Timing
Never seem to be in a hurry. Know when to wait. Anticipate the moments of opportunity.

## Law 36: Disdain Things You Cannot Have — Ignoring Them is the Best Revenge
The attempt to denigrate something draws more attention to it. Act with disdain and it loses power.

## Law 37: Create Compelling Spectacles
Striking imagery and grand symbolic gestures create the aura of power. Everyone responds to spectacle.

## Law 38: Think as You Like but Behave Like Others
If you make a display of your unconventional ideas, people will think you are trying to be different and will be suspicious. Blend in.

## Law 39: Stir Up Waters to Catch Fish
Make your opponents angry. When they are provoked, they lose their composure and make mistakes.

## Law 40: Despise the Free Lunch
Thefree lunch is usually a trap. Pay your own way. Control your own destiny.

## Law 41: Avoid Stepping into a Great Man's Shoes
The successor to a great achievement always lives in the shadow of the predecessor. Find your own path.

## Law 42: Strike the Shepherd and the Scatter the Sheep
Trouble can often be traced to a single strong individual. Remove the troublemaker and the group will stabilize.

## Law 43: Work on the Hearts and Minds of Others
Coercion creates a reaction that will eventually work against you. You must seduce others into wanting to move in your direction.

## Law 44: Disarm and Infuriate with the Mirror Effect
Mirror what your opponents do. Confuse them by reflecting their tactics back at them.

## Law 45: Preach the Need for Change, but Never Reform Too Much at Once
Everyone wants change but no one wants to be changed. Introduce change gradually.

## Law 46: Never Appear Too Perfect
Envy creates silent enemies. Appear flawed and human to deflect resentment.

## Law 47: Do Not Go Past the Mark You Aimed For — In Victory, Learn When to Stop
Overreach destroys the advantage gained. Know when to stop.

## Law 48: Assume Formlessness
By taking a shape, you become a target. Be fluid, adaptable, and formless. Never reveal your next move.
