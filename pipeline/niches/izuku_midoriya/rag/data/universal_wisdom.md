# Islamic Wisdom — Quran & Hadith (Key Teachings)

## On Patience (Sabr)
- "Verily, with hardship comes ease." (Quran 94:6)
- "And be patient, for indeed, Allah does not allow to be lost the reward of those who do good." (Quran 11:115)
- Patience is not passive — it is active endurance with faith.

## On Gratitude (Shukr)
- "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." (Quran 14:7)
- "The Prophet (ﷺ) used to pray until his feet swelled. When asked why, he said: 'Should I not be a grateful servant?'" (Bukhari)
- Gratitude multiplies blessings. Complaining diminishes them.

## On Knowledge (Ilm)
- "Seek knowledge from the cradle to the grave." (Hadith)
- "Whoever follows a path in pursuit of knowledge, Allah will make easy for them a path to Paradise." (Muslim)
- Knowledge is the greatest wealth. Seek it everywhere.

## On Character (Akhlaq)
- "The best among you are those who have the best character." (Bukhari)
- "I was sent to perfect good character." (Hadith)
- Character is not what you do when people watch — it's what you do when no one is watching.

## On Work and Effort
- "Allah loves that when one of you does a job, he does it with excellence (Ihsan)." (Hadith)
- "Tie your camel, then trust in Allah." (Hadith)
- Work hard. Prepare thoroughly. Then have faith.

## On Humility (Tawadu)
- "No one humbles himself for the sake of Allah but Allah raises him." (Muslim)
- "The arrogant will be gathered like atoms on the Day of Judgment." (Hadith)
- True greatness comes through service, not status.

## On Brotherhood and Community
- "The believers, in their mutual love, mercy, and compassion, are like one body." (Muslim)
- "Help your brother, whether he is an oppressor or is oppressed." (Bukhari)
- Community is sacred. Strengthen bonds. Forgive freely.

# Bible — Key Teachings (Proverbs & Wisdom)

## On Wisdom
- "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom." (Proverbs 9:10)
- "For wisdom is more precious than rubies, and nothing you desire can compare with her." (Proverbs 8:11)
- "If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God." (James 1:5)

## On Diligence
- "Go to the ant, you sluggard; consider its ways and be wise!" (Proverbs 6:6)
- "The hand of the diligent will rule, but the lazy man will be put to forced labor." (Proverbs 12:24)
- "Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord." (Colossians 3:23)

## On Humility
- "Pride goes before destruction, a haughty spirit before a fall." (Proverbs 16:18)
- "Humility comes before honor." (Proverbs 18:12)
- "Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit. Rather, in humility value others above yourselves." (Philippians 2:3)

## On Speech
- "The tongue has the power of life and death." (Proverbs 18:21)
- "A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger." (Proverbs 15:1)
- "Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt." (Colossians 4:6)

## On Character
- "A good name is more desirable than great riches." (Proverbs 22:1)
- "The integrity of the upright guides them, but the unfaithful are destroyed by their duplicity." (Proverbs 11:3)
- "Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it." (Proverbs 4:23)

# Buddhist Teachings — Dhammapada

## On the Mind
- "All that we are is the result of what we have thought. The mind is everything. What we think, we become." (Verse 1)
- "Hatred does not cease by hatred, but only by love; this is the eternal rule." (Verse 5)
- "An angry person is like a chariot going out of control — it is the anger that controls, not the person." (Verse 222)

## On Action
- "Better than a thousand hollow words is one word that brings peace." (Verse 100)
- "Better than a thousand useless verses is one line of the teaching that brings peace." (Verse 100)
- "If one speaks or acts with a pure mind, happiness follows like a shadow." (Verse 2)

## On Self-Discipline
- "No one saves us but ourselves. No one can and no one may. We ourselves must walk the path." (Verse 165)
- "Conquer anger with non-anger. Conquer evil with good. Conquer the stingy with generosity. Conquer the liar with truth." (Verse 223)

## On Mindfulness
- "The wise person protects the mind as a city is protected by its walls." (Verse 57)
- "Mindfulness is the path to the deathless. Negligence is the path to death." (Verse 21)

# Universal Wisdom Across Faiths

## The Golden Rule
- **Christianity:** "Do unto others as you would have them do unto you."
- **Islam:** "None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself."
- **Hinduism:** "This is the sum of duty: do not do to others what would cause pain if done to you."
- **Buddhism:** "Hurt not others in ways that you yourself would find hurtful."
- **Judaism:** "What is hateful to you, do not do to your neighbor."

## On Prayer and Meditation
Every tradition teaches: still the mind, connect with the divine, seek guidance, find peace.

## On Service
Every tradition teaches: serve others, give generously, put community above self.

## On Truth
Every tradition teaches: seek truth, live honestly, speak with integrity.

## On Compassion
Every tradition teaches: be kind, forgive freely, see the divine in all beings.
