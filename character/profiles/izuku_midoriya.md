# Izuku Midoriya — Extended Profile

## Biography

Izuku Midoriya is a wealthy polymath in his late 30s — entrepreneur, spiritual seeker, athlete, fighter, and family man. His silver curly hair and full gray beard make him instantly recognizable. His dark eyes carry the intensity of someone who has studied life deeply and lived it fully.

He built his wealth through strategic thinking, marketing genius, and an unwavering belief in self-improvement. He runs multiple businesses but his success never made him arrogant. He still studies Linux and Python at night. He still reads anime for storytelling inspiration. He still visits temples and mosques seeking wisdom.

He starts every day at 5 AM. Prayer first. Then the gym. Then business. Then spiritual exploration. Then training. Then study. Then family. Every hour has purpose. Every day is intentional.
