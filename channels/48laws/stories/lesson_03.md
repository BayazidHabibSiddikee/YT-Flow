# The Last Courtier

The kingdom had fallen, but the court remained.

Kael was the youngest advisor in three generations. He had risen quickly — too quickly. The King noticed. The Crown Prince noticed. The Queen noticed. And in this kingdom, being noticed by the wrong person was a death sentence.

Kael kept his ambitions hidden. He bowed low before the King, never offering advice unless asked. When the Crown Prince boasted of his victories, Kael listened and nodded, never contradicting. He became the man everyone forgot was in the room.

But when war came, it was Kael who won it.

Not through force. Through patience. Through the careful cultivation of alliances while others fought for glory. He never revealed his plans. He never showed his hand. When the enemy struck, Kael was already three moves ahead.

The King asked how he knew the enemy would come through the northern pass. Kael smiled and said nothing.

"Because," the King whispered to his general, "Kael is the only man in this court who never reveals his intentions."

By the time the kingdom understood Kael's power, it was too late to oppose him. He had become the King's shadow — essential, invisible, and indispensable.

**Law 3: Conceal your intentions. Keep people off-balance by never revealing the purpose behind your actions.**

The most powerful man in the kingdom was the one no one saw coming.
