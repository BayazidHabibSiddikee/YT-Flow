# The King's Shadow

Law 1: Never Outshine the Master

King Alaric was vain. He believed himself the greatest archer in the realm. Every courtier competed to be the best shot — and every one of them was executed for threatening the King's ego.

All except Kael.

When the King called for an archery contest, Kael stood at the back. He let the nobles boast. He let them hit the bullseye. When it was his turn, he shot wide of the target — deliberately.

"Ah, young Kael," the King boomed. "Still learning."

"Your Majesty is the finest archer who has ever lived," Kael replied, bowing low. "I can only hope to reach your level in another lifetime."

The King laughed and patted Kael's shoulder. "You shall. Stay by my side, and you will learn from the best."

That night, Kael studied the King's archery techniques in secret. Not to compete — to master them. To understand the weapon his master wielded.

Years later, when war came, Kael was the King's right hand. Not because he was the best shot — but because he made the King feel like the best shot.

**Law 1: Never outshine the master. Make those above you feel comfortably superior. In your desire to please and impress them, do not go too far in displaying your talents.**

The smartest man in the room is often the one who lets others think they are the smartest.
