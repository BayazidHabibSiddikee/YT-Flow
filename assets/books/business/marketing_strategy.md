# Marketing & Business Strategy — Key Frameworks

## The 4 P's of Marketing
1. **Product** — What you sell must solve a real problem
2. **Price** — Value-based pricing, not cost-based
3. **Place** — Be where your customers are
4. **Promotion** — Tell the right story to the right people

## Blue Ocean Strategy
Don't compete in crowded markets (red oceans). Create uncontested market space (blue oceans). Make the competition irrelevant.

### Four Actions Framework
1. **Eliminate** — What factors should the industry eliminate?
2. **Reduce** — What factors should be reduced below the standard?
3. **Raise** — What factors should be raised above the standard?
4. **Create** — What factors should be created that the industry never offered?

## The Lean Startup
1. **Build** — Create a minimum viable product (MVP)
2. **Measure** — Collect data on customer behavior
3. **Learn** — Decide to pivot or persevere

## Growth Hacking
- **AARRR Framework:** Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral
- **Product-Market Fit:** When the product satisfies strong market demand
- **Viral Loops:** Users bring in more users automatically

## Personal Branding
- **Be consistent** across all platforms
- **Tell stories**, not facts
- **Provide value** before asking for anything
- **Be authentic** — people detect fakeness instantly

## Content Marketing
- **Educate** — Teach something valuable
- **Entertain** — Make people enjoy consuming your content
- **Inspire** — Move people to action
- **Sell** — Only after providing value

## Social Media Psychology
- People share what makes them look good
- Emotions drive sharing (awe, anger, joy, fear)
- Visual content outperforms text
- Consistency beats virality

## Negotiation Mastery
1. **BATNA** — Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement
2. **Anchoring** — Set the frame early
3. **Mirroring** — Repeat their last 3 words to build rapport
4. **Labeling** — Name their emotions to disarm them
5. **Calibrated Questions** — "How am I supposed to do that?"

## The Psychology of Sales
- People buy emotionally and justify logically
- Scarcity increases desire
- Social proof reduces risk perception
- Authority builds trust
- Reciprocity creates obligation

## Scaling a Business
- **Systematize** — Document every process
- **Automate** — Use technology for repetitive tasks
- **Delegate** — Hire for your weaknesses
- **Measure** — What gets measured gets improved
- **Focus** — Do fewer things better

## Financial Literacy for Entrepreneurs
- **Revenue** is vanity, **profit** is sanity, **cash** is king
- Know your numbers: CAC, LTV, margins, burn rate
- Reinvest in growth, but maintain reserves
- Multiple revenue streams = financial resilience
