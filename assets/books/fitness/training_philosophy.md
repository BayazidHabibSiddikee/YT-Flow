# Can't Hurt Me — David Goggins (Key Lessons)

## The 40% Rule
When your mind tells you you're done, you're only 40% done. The human body can endure far more than the mind thinks possible.

## The Cookie Jar
Keep a mental jar of past victories. When things get hard, reach in and remember what you've already overcome.

## Accountability Mirror
Look yourself in the eye every morning and be brutally honest. What are you doing? What could you be doing better?

## The Mirror Theory
There are two mirrors: the accountability mirror and the comparison mirror. Never compare yourself to others — only to who you were yesterday.

## Taking Souls
When you outwork everyone, you break their spirit. Excellence is the ultimate form of competition.

## The Light Switch
Turn it on or off. Commit fully or don't start at all. There is no half-measures.

## Suffering Is a Moment
Pain is temporary. Quitting lasts forever. The suffering you endure today builds the person you become tomorrow.

## Goggins' Rules for Life
1. Callous your mind through hardship
2. Take souls through relentless excellence
3. Be the uncommon man among uncommon men
4. Power of the mind is infinite

# Extreme Ownership — Jocko Willink

## Principle 1: There Are No Bad Teams, Only Bad Leaders
If your team fails, you failed as a leader. Own it.

## Principle 2: Believe in the Mission
If you don't believe in what you're doing, neither will your team. Conviction is contagious.

## Principle 3: Remove Ego
Ego clouds judgment. Check your ego at the door.

## Principle 4: Lead Up and Down the Chain
Make sure your superiors are informed and your subordinates are empowered.

## Principle 5: Discipline Equals Freedom
The more disciplined you are in the fundamentals, the more freedom you have in everything else.

# Training Philosophy

## Consistency Over Intensity
Showing up every day matters more than occasional heroic efforts. Build the habit first.

## Progressive Overload
Gradually increase the demands on your body. Small, consistent improvements compound over time.

## Recovery Is Training
Sleep, nutrition, rest days — these are when you actually grow. Training is just the stimulus.

## Mental Toughness
The body achieves what the mind believes. Train your mind alongside your body.

## Deliberate Practice
Don't just go through the motions. Every rep, every set, every session should have intention.

## Compound Movements
Squats, deadlifts, bench press, overhead press, rows — these build the foundation. Isolation comes after.

## Cardio for Longevity
Heart health is everything. Run, swim, bike — keep your cardiovascular system strong.

## Nutrition Fundamentals
- Protein for muscle repair
- Carbs for energy
- Fats for hormones
- Calories in vs. calories out
- Whole foods > supplements
