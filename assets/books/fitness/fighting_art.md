# Fighting & Self-Defense — Principles

## Boxing Fundamentals
- **Stance:** Feet shoulder-width apart, dominant foot back, hands up protecting chin
- **Jab:** Fast, straight, sets up everything else. Your most important punch.
- **Cross:** Power hand, rotate hips and shoulders for maximum force.
- **Hook:** Short, powerful, comes from the side. Target the jaw.
- **Uppercut:** Close range, comes from below. Target the chin or body.
- **Defense:** Slip, bob and weave, parry, block. Never stand still.
- **Footwork:** Move constantly, cut angles, never cross your feet.

## Muay Thai Basics
- **The Eight Limbs:** Fists, elbows, knees, shins — all weapons.
- **Teep (Push Kick):** Controls distance, disrupts opponent's rhythm.
- **Roundhouse Kick:** Power comes from hip rotation, not leg strength.
- **Clinch:** Close-range fighting, knees and elbows.
- **Checking Kicks:** Lift your shin to block incoming kicks.

## Self-Defense Principles
1. **Awareness** — The best fight is the one avoided. Stay alert.
2. **Distance** — Keep space between you and the threat.
3. **De-escalation** — Walk away if possible. Winning a fight you didn't need is still a loss.
4. **Escape** — Your goal is to get away, not to win.
5. **Improvised Weapons** — Keys, pens, bags — anything can be a weapon.
6. **Target Vulnerabilities** — Eyes, throat, groin, knees. Soft targets for survival.
7. **Run** — After creating distance, run. Don't look back.

## Mental Aspects of Fighting
- **Fear is natural** — Use it as fuel, not paralysis.
- **Control your breathing** — Slow breaths calm the nervous system.
- **Commit to your strikes** — Hesitation gets you hit.
- **Stay calm** — Panic wastes energy and clouds judgment.
- **See the openings** — Patience creates opportunities.

## Training Philosophy
- **Train how you fight** — Practice under realistic conditions.
- **Spar regularly** — Technique without application is theory.
- **Condition your body** — Strong neck, iron shins, powerful core.
- **Study footage** — Watch fights, analyze techniques.
- **Be humble** — Everyone gets beat. Learn from every loss.

## Physical Conditioning for Fighters
- **Cardio** — Roadwork, jump rope, swimming. Endurance is survival.
- **Strength** — Compound lifts, functional movements, core work.
- **Flexibility** — Stretch daily. Injury prevention and range of motion.
- **Reaction drills** — Partner drills, mitt work, bag work.
- **Mental toughness** — Push through discomfort. Embrace the grind.

## The Fighter's Mindset
- You don't rise to the occasion — you fall to the level of your training.
- Every opponent teaches you something.
- The fight is won or lost before it begins — in the gym, in the mind.
- Respect your opponent, but don't fear them.
- The best fighters are the best students.
